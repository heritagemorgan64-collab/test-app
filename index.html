Upload one or two videos. The scanner samples frames at a configurable interval, processes them one at a time, and keeps separate evidence per video. OCR runs in your browser using Tesseract.js.

What the scanner can and cannot guarantee

Video frame sampling cannot guarantee every row will be captured. OCR guesses must be reviewed. OCR score filtering now accepts only single-digit goal pairs (0–9 per team) to reject common odds/time readings such as 14-8. It still uses general text/score heuristics, not a trained betting-slip layout model or a live sports database. It does not verify outcomes externally.
